Formal labour market in Santa Catarina, Brazil

How did employment, turnover and wages change in the state from 2019 to 2025, and where are the gaps?

  • +27% formal jobs in six years and +8% median real wage, which only passed its 2019 level in 2022.
  • Turnover rose from 53% to 68%, but turnover net of quits stayed flat at ~34%: quits went from 30% to 42% of all separations. A tight labour market.
  • Women earn 83% to 87% of men's median wage in every region. In the capital's region, Black and pardo workers earn 70% of the white median.
In the reviewI replaced partial CSV extracts with the complete RAIS (Brazil's annual census of formal jobs) on BigQuery and kept the star schema at contract grain, because a median cannot be rebuilt from aggregates. A test caught a coding change in the source in 2023.

23 years of a university entrance exam

How many people apply to the Federal University of Santa Catarina's entrance exam, for which courses, and what has changed since 2003?

  • Applicants fell 52% (40,124 in 2005 → 19,178 in 2025) while vacancies grew 12%. Competition went from 10.2 to 4.4 applicants per vacancy.
  • Engineering −77% since 2013. Civil Engineering −88% (2,058 → 243). Teaching degrees −78%.
  • Health holds up (−22%) and Computing has been growing again since 2013.
In the reviewI rebuilt the series from about 200 HTML and PDF files in 5 different layouts, reconciled with each year's official total (95–98%). The text now makes clear that part of the vacancies moved to the national ENEM-based system, so fewer exam applicants is not the same as less demand for the university.

Beyond FIFA Rankings

Which national team was playing best going into the 2026 World Cup, judging by how it plays, not just by results?

  • A score with 5 pillars (attack, defense, control, differential and efficiency), built from z-scores of the last 20 matches of 29 teams.
  • Each match is weighted by the opponent's strength (Elo): playing Spain counts 2.25× more than playing Ukraine.
  • Spain, England, Germany and Morocco at the top. Brazil is 15th: it defends well but ranks 23rd in attack.
In the reviewBefore changing anything, I proved the refactored code reproduced the published ranking with a 0.00 difference. Then I fixed two bugs (missing data counted as zero; wrong Elo codes). The top 4 held.

NBA Analytics Pipeline

An end-to-end pipeline: from the official NBA API to a dashboard that compares players and teams with the league average.

  • Python → BigQuery → Dataform → Power BI, with one command: python main.py.
  • 2025–26 season: 26,651 player games, 1,230 games and 582 players. A test reconciles players' points with each team's score.

Web scraping + geoReal estate

Does the beach set the price in Florianópolis?

How much is being near the sea worth? I analyzed 1,514 apartments for sale, measuring the distance to the real outline of 24 beaches.

  • Within 1 km of the beach, the price per m² is ~21% higher (median R$ 18.2k vs. R$ 15.1k between 1 and 5 km).
  • But distance explains little: Beira-Mar Norte and Agronômica are among the most expensive neighborhoods, 10 km from any ocean beach, because they face the bay.
  • And Ingleses is 400 m from the sea with one of the lowest prices per m² in the city.
In the reviewI found that the listings API already returned coordinates, so the geocoding step (11% failures) was unnecessary. I replaced each beach point with its OpenStreetMap outline, measured in meters (UTM 22S).

I revisited my old projects

Before building this portfolio, I rebuilt every project so that anyone can run it. Along the way I found errors, some of them in analyses I had already posted on LinkedIn. Instead of hiding them, I documented each one in the repository README, with the before and after.

Labour market

The +52% "break" in 2022 employment was in the extract: the files had only ~60% of the contracts. Real growth was 6.5%.

UFSC

A range() skipped 2007 and 2010, and recent years included practice-only candidates. Applicants fell 52%, not 41%.

World Cup

Scotland had the Elo code of the Seychelles, and missing statistics counted as zero. Nigeria moved up 5 places.

Real estate

18% of listings had their distance to the beach off by more than 1 km: each beach was a single point, and Jurerê was not even on the list.

NBA

The tests found 5 neutral-site games (Las Vegas, Mexico City, Berlin, London) that broke the home/away rule.

How I work

Three habits I brought from construction sites to data.

Reproducible

Anyone can clone, set a .env and run. No project IDs or local paths in the code, and source data is versioned or has a download script.

Tested

Uniqueness and integrity tests, and above all reconciliation tests: players' points against the team's, initial stock + hires − separations against active jobs, a rebuilt series against the official total.

Honest with the data

Medians instead of means when there are outliers, caveats written in the README, and what the data does not say made as clear as what it does.
